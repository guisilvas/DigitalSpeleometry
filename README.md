# Digital Speleometry — Study Roadmap

## Math

- 3D analytic geometry: points, vectors, distances, lines, and planes | [MIT — Multivariable Calculus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-02sc-multivariable-calculus-fall-2010/) |
- Linear algebra: matrices, rotations, linear transformations, eigenvalues, and SVD | [MIT — Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/) |
- Probability and statistics: mean, variance, covariance, noise, and regression | [MIT — Probability and Statistics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/) |
- Metrology and uncertainty propagation | [NIST — Measurement Uncertainty](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed/topic-areas/measurement-uncertainty) |

---

## Physics

- Geometric optics and image formation | [OpenCV — Camera Calibration](https://docs.opencv.org/5.x/dc/dbb/tutorial_py_calibration.html) |
- Photogrammetry, triangulation, and 3D reconstruction | [OpenCV — 3D Reconstruction](https://docs.opencv.org/2.4.0/modules/calib3d/doc/camera_calibration_and_3d_reconstruction.html) |
- Laser sensing and distance measurement | [Idrees & Pradhan (2016) — Cave Surveying with Laser Scanning](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/ijs/vol45/iss1/8/) |
- Coordinate systems and spatial transformations | [Open3D — ICP Registration](https://open3d.org/html/tutorial/pipelines/icp_registration.html) |
- Precision, accuracy, calibration, and measurement errors | [NIST — Measurement Uncertainty](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed/topic-areas/measurement-uncertainty) |

## Digital Speleometry

- Cave surveying using terrestrial laser scanning | [Idrees & Pradhan (2016)](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/ijs/vol45/iss1/8/) |
- 3D cave mapping using open-source software | [Cosso, Ferrando & Orlando (2014)](https://isprs-archives.copernicus.org/articles/XL-5/181/2014/) |
- High-resolution 3D mapping and cave geomorphology | [Fabbri et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2016.12.001) |
- Speleological mapping using photogrammetry in Minas Gerais, Brazil | [Brazilian Journal of Speleology](https://revistaeletronica.icmbio.gov.br/rebe/article/view/2319) |
