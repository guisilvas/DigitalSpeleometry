## Papers

1. **Automação de processos no mapeamento espeleológico 3D a partir de modelos fotogramétricos de cavidades subterrâneas em Minas Gerais, Brasil (2022)**
   - **Focus:** Photogrammetry, automation, cave measurements, cross-sections, and volume estimation.
   - **Link:** https://revistaeletronica.icmbio.gov.br/rebe/article/view/2319
   - **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.37002/rbesp.v1i11.2319

2. **High-resolution 3-D mapping using terrestrial laser scanning as a tool for geomorphological and speleogenetical studies in caves (2017)**
   - **Authors:** Fabbri et al.
   - **Focus:** Point clouds, 3D reconstruction, volume estimation, and cave wall roughness analysis.
   - **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2016.12.001

3. **Surveying and mapping a cave using 3D laser scanner: the open challenge with free and open source software (2014)**
   - **Authors:** Cosso, Ferrando, and Orlando.
   - **Focus:** Point cloud registration, filtering, mesh generation, and open-source tools.
   - **Link:** https://isprs-archives.copernicus.org/articles/XL-5/181/2014/

4. **Geomorphological and speleogenetical observations using terrestrial laser scanning and 3D photogrammetry in a gypsum cave (2018)**
   - **Focus:** Comparison of terrestrial laser scanning (TLS) and photogrammetry for cave morphology measurements.
   - **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2018.07.012

5. **Integrated high-precision real scene 3D modeling of karst cave landscape based on laser scanning and photogrammetry (2024)**
   - **Focus:** Integration of laser scanning and photogrammetry for 3D cave modeling.
   - **Link:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11372102/

6. **Gypsum cave notches and their palaeoenvironmental significance: A combined morphometric study using terrestrial laser scanning, traditional cave mapping, and geomorphological observations (2025)**
   - **Focus:** Morphometric analysis, surface fitting, spatial statistics, and 3D point clouds.
   - **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2024.109576

7. **Terrestrial laser scanning for 3D mapping of an alpine ice cave (2024)**
   - **Focus:** Point cloud registration and processing, geometric accuracy, and principal component analysis (PCA).
   - **Link:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10962254/

8. **Mapeamento topográfico de cavernas no setor mineral: avanços e novas tecnologias (2023)**
   - **Focus:** Cave surveying technologies, 3D laser scanning, and photogrammetry in Brazil.
   - **Link:** https://doi.org/10.20502/rbg.v24i3.2315
